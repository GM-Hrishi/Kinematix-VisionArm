# Credits

## Mechanical design

The 3D-printed arm chassis (links, joints, and gripper mechanism) used in this
project is based on the **"Robotic Arm with Servo & Arduino"** design by
**Emre Kalem**, published on MakerWorld:

https://makerworld.com/en/models/1134925-robotic-arm-with-servo-arduino

We printed the parts largely as-is (link lengths: lower arm 242mm, upper arm
172mm), so the arm's reach and geometry follow Emre Kalem's original design.
All firmware, wiring, control logic, vision pipeline, and simulation work in
this repo are our own, built on top of that mechanical base.

Thank you to Emre Kalem for making the design freely available.
